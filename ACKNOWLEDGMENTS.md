# Acknowledgments & Research Foundations

The **Universal Frontier Engineering Harness** stands on the shoulders of cutting-edge agentic software engineering and LLM cognitive science research. We gratefully acknowledge the insights, papers, and open-source projects that informed this work.

---

## Community Projects & Prior Art

- **[Gravitas by Uday Sharma (@udaysharmadev)](https://github.com/udaysharmadev/Gravitas)**:
  We acknowledge Gravitas for its academic-grade failure mode taxonomy and for demonstrating the efficacy of host interceptors (`Stop` hooks). We adapted and hardened these concepts into an ultra-low latency, cross-platform engine with strict UTF-8 handling.

- **[SWE-agent (Princeton NLP)](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/SWE-agent)**:
  For pioneering the **Agent-Computer Interface (ACI)** and proving that software engineering agents require structured, guarded interaction layers rather than raw, unconstrained terminal access.

---

## Academic Literature & Research Foundations

1. **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models** (Yao et al., ICLR 2023)  
   *Grounding reasoning traces in verifiable actions and environment observations to suppress hallucination.*

2. **Plan-and-Solve Prompting** (Wang et al., ACL 2023)  
   *Explicit problem decomposition prior to code execution to prevent compounding multi-step errors.*

3. **Chain-of-Verification (CoVe)** (Dhuliawala et al., ACL 2024)  
   *Independent, unbiased verification questions to counter self-congratulatory rationalization.*

4. **Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning** (Shinn et al., NeurIPS 2023)  
   *Persistent failure logging to prevent dead-end repetition.*

5. **METR Time Horizons** (METR, 2026)  
   *Empirical study of error compounding and context degradation as agent operational horizons extend.*

6. **Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts** (Liu et al., TACL 2024)  
   *Demonstration of the U-shaped recall curve and attention decay across mid-context instruction blocks.*
